For agencies and production houses

Production teams and traffic systems

I set up video production departments for advertising agencies and production houses: who does what, how a brief travels from the first call to the final master, and how each job is priced, tracked and checked. At the centre is a traffic system the whole team works from.

  • Seven stages from brief to archive, with three quality gates
  • One live tracker for every job, owner, deadline and piece of kit
  • Budgets built before the idea is sold, in lean, hybrid and full versions
  • A scored vendor bench, coded equipment and a skills matrix for training

Production Desk is the app I built to run it. Managers assign the work, the team accepts and delivers stage by stage, and every closed job feeds a performance score.

For brands and agencies

Creative proposals that sell the idea

A good idea still has to be bought. I write and design the proposal that sells it, so a brand can see the film before a single shoot day is booked.

  • The insight, the idea and the script, chapter by chapter
  • AI key visuals made with the real product, cast and locations
  • Looks, wardrobe, camera and light, set out for the client
  • Deliverables, shoot days and a post plan the client can sign off

Example: Just the Two of Us, a 60-second hero film in five chapters for the LYNK & CO 900, planned as a two-day shoot in Ras Al Khaimah.
